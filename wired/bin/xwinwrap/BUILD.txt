xwinwrap — vendored for X11 gifpapers
==========================================
xwinwrap pins a window to the desktop layer so mpv can play animated
wallpapers behind everything on X11 (the equivalent of mpvpaper on Wayland).

Not packaged in Debian — vendored here instead.

Source: https://github.com/r00tdaemon/xwinwrap (fork; gif-background use case)
Build:  gcc -o xwinwrap xwinwrap.c -lX11 -lXext -lXrender
        (needs libx11-dev; binary is ~30KB)

The committed binary is x86_64. Rebuild for ARM when the ARM image lands.

Used by: gifpaperslain (X11 branch) -> /usr/bin/xwinwrap
